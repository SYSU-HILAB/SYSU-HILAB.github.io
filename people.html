Biography

Ximin Lyu received his B.Eng. (2012) and M.Phil. (2014) degrees in Aircraft Manufacturing from Harbin Institute of Technology, and his Ph.D. (2019) degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. He worked as a Senior Flight Control Researcher at DJI in 2018. He is currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Intelligent Systems Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University (Shenzhen). His research focuses on robotics and control, especially UAV/UGV design, control, and planning.
